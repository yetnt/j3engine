# Mathematics

This is just a little explainer on certain maths concepts

## Vector3

A Vector3 in J3Engine's terms is an object with an X, Y and Z magnitude of type double.
Mathematically, a Vector3 is an ordered triplet of numbers (x, y, z) that represents a point in 3D space relative to an origin, or a displacement from one point to another. It has both magnitude (length) and direction.

In computer graphics, Vector3s are fundamental. They are used to:

---

**Represent** **Positions:** The coordinates (x, y, z) of an object or vertex in 3D space.

---

**Define** **Directions:** A normalized vector (magnitude of 1) indicating a direction, such as a light ray, camera 
view direction, or the orientation of a surface.

---

**Store** **Normals:** Vectors perpendicular to a surface, crucial for lighting calculations to determine how light reflects.
